# Immersive Product Configurator

## Goal

Let visitors convince themselves with their own data: an interactive configurator, simulator, calculator or comparison with premium quality and honest logic.

## Use when

- Products with options, plans, customization, ROI, 3D visualization or comparisons (SaaS, high-ticket e-commerce, B2B services, real estate, education, consulting).

## Requirements

1. Inputs, a useful result, the follow-up CTA with context, and a disclaimer when estimates are involved.
2. Controls that fit the data: sliders, toggles, steppers, swatches, tabs, or canvas/3D.
3. Instant feedback, validation, thumb-friendly mobile layout, keyboard and screen reader access, light persistence (URL or local storage), stable performance.
4. Transparent, testable logic with visible assumptions.

## Limits

- No financial calculations without a source or a clear notice; never hide assumptions; no complex interaction without conversion benefit.
