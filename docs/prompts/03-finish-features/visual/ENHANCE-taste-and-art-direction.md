# Taste and Art Direction

## Goal

Replace generic, framework-default styling with a deliberate point of view and a repeatable editorial system, so the page feels authored instead of anonymous.

## Use when

- The page is correct but anonymous; before adding motion or 3D when the visual language is missing.
- Portfolios, agencies, premium brands, complex products and case studies.

## Requirements

1. **Tokens first:** build or unify the token system (from `DESIGN.md` when it exists) in the global CSS or theme config.
2. **Relational color:** muted, tinted bases (e.g. `hsl(220 15% 8%)` dark or warm linen `hsl(40 30% 98%)` light) instead of raw framework colors; surfaces separated by small lightness steps; refined accents (muted metallics, deep cobalt, matte emerald), used sparingly.
3. **Typography:** one modular scale (e.g. 1.25 or 1.414); tighter tracking on large headings, relaxed leading on long text; strong contrast between display and body sizes.
4. **Editorial system:** grid, vertical rhythm, image treatment, how numbers, claims and proof are presented; controlled asymmetric layouts; sections with tension, pauses and payoffs.
5. **Containers:** hairline borders at low opacity, crisp layered shadows with a clear light direction instead of heavy blur, restrained glass (backdrop blur plus a subtle border) only for floating UI.
6. Keep CTAs visible, hierarchy clear and reading scannable.

## Beyond the checklist

Think of a luxury magazine with commercial goals. External taste skills can help (`helen skills external taste-skill`), but use only one main design skill.

## Limits

- Never sacrifice clarity or contrast for experimental composition; do not fill everything with cards; no lorem ipsum or AI filler.
- Do not change the brand identity without confirmation.

## Output

```text

Changes applied:
- 1-3 bullets with the exact changes

Manual actions:
- None. / what the user must do (e.g. set a variable, provide real images)
```
