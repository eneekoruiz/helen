# 07 · Client handoff

The last mile before a demo, publication or delivery, and a handoff package the recipient can operate without you.

## Quick decisions

- Full last-mile review and handoff: `apply-client-delivery-flow`.
- Copy or claims doubts: `audit-content-copy-brand-and-claims`.
- Broken paths risk: `audit-links-forms-ctas-and-conversion-paths`.
- About to demo or share a link: `audit-browser-smoke-test-and-demo-readiness`.
- Images and media: `audit-media-assets-alt-text-and-performance`.
- Stop: never deliver with fake data, placeholders or credentials exposed.

## Exit checklist

Before moving on to 08-maintenance:

- [ ] Content is real, approved and free of placeholders.
- [ ] Every CTA, link and form works on desktop and mobile.
- [ ] The browser smoke test is READY.
- [ ] Media has alt text and reasonable weight.
- [ ] The handoff package documents setup, deploy, access (where, not what) and support.
- [ ] The client signed off, or open items are explicit.
