# Awwwards and Site of the Year Visual Excellence Audit

Purpose: review a public-facing web experience against a very high bar for visual craft, interaction quality, originality, and memorability.

## Prompt

Act as an Awwwards jury member, Site of the Year reviewer, creative director, motion designer, senior frontend engineer, and product-minded founder.

Review this website or web app as if it were being considered for a high-end digital design award.

## Requisitos mínimos obligatorios

1. Review first impression, concept, art direction, visual identity, layout rhythm, typography, color, imagery, interaction, animation, transitions, responsiveness, and performance perception.
2. Check whether the experience has a memorable idea or only polished components.
3. Evaluate scroll behavior, microinteractions, hover/focus states, page transitions, loading, storytelling, and content choreography.
4. Review technical craft: smoothness, responsive framing, asset quality, accessibility basics, text readability, and absence of visual glitches.
5. Identify what prevents the project from feeling award-caliber.

## Más allá de estos criterios

Think like a demanding creative reviewer. Look for taste, restraint, surprise, detail density, narrative rhythm, originality, and emotional impact.

Separate award-level ambition from product usability. If an effect damages clarity, performance, accessibility, or conversion, call it out.

Do not recommend gratuitous animation, heavy assets, or trendy effects unless they serve the concept.

## Output format

1. Award-readiness verdict.
2. First-impression critique.
3. Visual craft issues.
4. Interaction and motion issues.
5. Originality and memorability gaps.
6. Technical craft blockers.
7. Highest-leverage improvements.
8. Verdict: `AWARD-CALIBER`, `STRONG BUT NOT SOTY`, or `NOT AWARDS-READY`.
