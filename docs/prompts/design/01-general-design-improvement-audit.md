# General Design Improvement Audit

Purpose: improve the overall design quality of a product without requiring a full redesign.

## Prompt

Act as a senior Product Designer, UI Engineer, UX Researcher, design systems reviewer, and founder with strong taste.

Review the product's overall design quality.

## Requisitos mínimos obligatorios

1. Review layout, hierarchy, spacing, typography, density, color, contrast, icons, affordances, motion, responsive behavior, and state design.
2. Check whether the visual system matches the product category, audience, and level of seriousness.
3. Identify inconsistencies across screens, components, docs, screenshots, and public assets.
4. Review empty, loading, error, success, disabled, hover, focus, and destructive states.
5. Identify changes that improve clarity without making the product more decorative or complex.

## Más allá de estos criterios

Use design judgment. Look for what makes the product feel generic, template-like, unfinished, visually noisy, emotionally flat, or less trustworthy than it should.

Recommend improvements that make the product calmer, clearer, more coherent, more memorable, and more premium.

Do not redesign everything unless the current visual system is the actual problem.

## Output format

1. Biggest design quality issues.
2. High-impact improvements.
3. Component/system inconsistencies.
4. State design gaps.
5. Quick wins.
6. Larger design decisions.
7. Verdict: `DESIGN SOLID`, `DESIGN NEEDS POLISH`, or `DESIGN HOLD`.
