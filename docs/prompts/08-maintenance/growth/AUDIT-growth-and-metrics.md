# Growth Model and Metrics Audit

## Goal

Understand how the product attracts, activates, retains and expands users, and define the smallest measurement system that would actually change decisions.

## Use when

- After launch, when growth stalls, or before adding analytics.

## Requirements

1. **Growth model:** acquisition, activation, retention, referral and conversion paths; upgrade moments, sharing loops, onboarding nudges, reactivation.
2. **Value without continuation:** where users get value but are not guided to the next step.
3. **Metrics:** activation, retention, conversion, quality, reliability and support; north-star candidates; vanity metrics to drop.
4. **Events:** the event map needed, and what is missing today.
5. **Privacy:** consent, data minimization, what not to track.
6. Growth claims without evidence.

## Beyond the checklist

Low-cost leverage: better examples, shareable output, templates, public artifacts, SEO pages, invite loops, lifecycle emails, clearer conversion moments. Never growth mechanics that damage trust.

## Limits

- Audit only. No analytics that create privacy risk or operational burden without clear value.

## Output

1. Current growth model and north-star candidates.
2. Findings grouped as **Critical**, **Important** and **Optional**. For each: evidence (file, line, screen or command), impact, recommended fix and effort.
3. Event map, privacy constraints, a decision dashboard proposal and recommended experiments.
