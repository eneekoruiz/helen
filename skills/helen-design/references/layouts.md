# Portfolio and showcase layout patterns

Patterns observed in reference pieces. Extract the principle; never copy a site's design or text.

1. **Sandwich**: large centered name on top, featured work in the middle (cards slightly rotated or overlapping), discipline or role at the bottom. Instant presence; needs excellent images.
2. **Showcase (work first)**: no fluff. One short positioning line, then straight to a row or grid of projects (horizontal scroll works well). Best when the work speaks for itself.
3. **Sticky split**: left column fixed with name, role, short "about" and links; right column a scrolling grid of projects. Premium feel and clear hierarchy. Collapse to one column with a compact header on mobile.

Decide by real material (how many projects, how strong), brand tone, and goal (hiring, selling, prestige). Full prompt: `generate-portfolio-layout-patterns`.
